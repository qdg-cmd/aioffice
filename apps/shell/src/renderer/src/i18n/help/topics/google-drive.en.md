# Google Drive

Connect your Google Drive to open and edit Drive files in GenOffice. When you save, the file goes back to Drive. You can also let the AI in the editors find and read files in your Drive.

## Connecting

1. Open **Settings ▸ Integrations** and click **Connect Google Drive**.
2. Your browser opens Google's sign-in page. Sign in and allow access.
3. Go back to GenOffice. The card shows the account you connected.

The sign-in is kept on this PC, encrypted with Windows' own protection for your account. If this PC cannot encrypt it, the connection lasts until you close the app.

## Opening Drive files

- On Home, click **Google Drive** in the sidebar. Switch between **My Drive**, **Shared with me** and **Recent**, or search by name and content.
- Click a file to open it in its editor. Files GenOffice cannot open are greyed out.
- Google Docs, Sheets and Slides open as Word, Excel and PowerPoint files.

## Saving back to Drive

- Save as usual. A moment later the file is uploaded to Drive.
- If someone changed the file in Drive after you opened it, GenOffice asks whether to overwrite it in Drive, save a copy to Drive, or keep your change on this PC only.
- A file you can only view is saved to Drive as a copy, or kept on this PC.
- A Google document is left as it is. Your changes are saved as an Office file in the same folder.
- If the upload fails, your changes stay on this PC and GenOffice tries again, also the next time it starts.

## The AI and your Drive

- In the AI panel of the Word, Excel, PowerPoint, HTML, mind map and Hangul editors, **Attach from Google Drive** (next to the attach button) opens a window where you tick Drive files or folders. They are attached like files from this PC; a folder brings up to 20 of its files.
- While **Let the AI find and read files in Drive** is on (it is on once you connect), the AI in every editor can search your Drive and read files. For example: "Find last month's meeting notes in Drive and summarize them."
- What the AI reads goes to the AI provider you use. Parts hidden from AI stay hidden.
- The AI cannot change, delete or share Drive files.
- Once a chat holds something read from Drive, web search, image search, reading a page by its web address, and pictures by web address stop working in that chat. Start a new chat to use them again.

## Disconnecting

Click **Disconnect** in **Settings ▸ Integrations**. GenOffice removes the sign-in from this PC and asks Google to revoke its access. Copies of files already on this PC stay.
